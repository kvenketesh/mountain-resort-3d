# 🏔️ ALPINESCAPE — A Couple's Mountain, Lake & Resort Journey

A cinematic, interactive 3D WebGL web application capturing a couple's full romantic 24-hour getaway experience in an alpine mountain resort with an infinity swimming pool adjoining a pristine lake.

---

## 🌟 The Experiential Storyline (4 Chapters)

1. **🌅 Chapter 1: Morning Awakening & Lakeside Breakfast (07:30 AM)**
   - Sun rising over the mountain ridges with golden dawn light and mist skimming the alpine lake.
   - The couple sits on the sunlit resort deck right beside the adjoining infinity swimming pool.
   - Enjoying fresh gourmet breakfast: steaming espresso mugs, warm croissants, fresh berry bowl, and wildflowers.
   - **Interactive Activities**: Clink espresso cups (with toast clink sound & heart confetti), savor warm croissants, gaze at the wooden rowboat and gliding white swans.

2. **⛰️ Chapter 2: The Ascent & Breathtaking Summit (01:30 PM)**
   - Journeying up via the **scenic aerial Cable Car / Gondola** to **Cloudcrest Peak** at 7,200 ft.
   - Standing hand-in-hand at the rustic timber overlook railing.
   - 360° panoramic vista of snow-capped peaks, fluttering prayer flags, soaring golden eagles, and the resort lake shimmering thousands of feet below.
   - **Interactive Activities**: First-person 24x optical zoom Telescope Viewfinder, ride inside the animated red alpine cable car, and track the soaring mountain eagle.

3. **✨ Chapter 3: Nightfall, The Starry Canopy & Poolside Glow (09:30 PM)**
   - Returning to the resort as twilight transitions to velvet night.
   - Over 3,500 twinkling stars, the Milky Way band, and dynamic shooting stars across the cosmos.
   - The couple relaxes at the illuminated infinity pool edge with submerged cyan LEDs casting shimmering reflections into the dark adjoining lake, surrounded by gentle luminescent fireflies.
   - **Interactive Activities**: Take a night dip / swim in the warm pool with submerged floating cocktail tray, map celestial constellations (The Dipper, Orion, & Romantic Heart of the Alps), roast marshmallows by the sunken lakeside campfire.

4. **🛏️ Chapter 4: Cozy Cabin Sanctuary & Drifting to Bed (11:30 PM)**
   - Retiring into the master suite with panoramic floor-to-ceiling glass windows.
   - Warm ambient lighting and glowing embers from the stone fireplace.
   - Resting together in the plush king-size bed under a down duvet, watching the moonbeam glisten on the lake until drifting to sleep.
   - **Interactive Activities**: Stoke the fireplace embers with glowing sparks, play calming acoustic bedtime sleep chords, and gaze out at the moonlit lake.

---

## 🎮 New & Expanded Interactive Features

- **🚡 Scenic Alpine Cable Car / Gondola**: Real-time animated gondola car traveling along mountain ridge cables between the valley resort and the summit terminal. Includes a **Ride Cable Car** POV mode!
- **🦅 Living Alpine Fauna**: Soaring golden eagle circling the high peaks with animated flapping wings, and graceful white swans swimming on the lake with subtle ripple wakes.
- **🔥 Sunken Lakeside Firepit Lounge**: Outdoor circular stone lounge beside the pool with campfire logs, flickering fire, and roasting marshmallows.
- **🌦️ Dynamic Alpine Weather Toolbar**:
  - ☀️ **Alpine Sunshine**: Crisp lighting and mountain shadows.
  - 🌫️ **Lake Mist & Fog**: Ethereal morning fog skimming the water.
  - 🌧️ **Mountain Rain**: Realistic falling rain streaks with cozy rooftop rain audio synthesis.
  - ❄️ **Winter Snow Flurries**: Soft drifting crystalline snowflakes.
  - 🌈 **Alpine Rainbow**: Post-rain rainbow arc over the valley.
- **✨ Constellation Stargazer**: Toggle glowing celestial constellation lines (Ursa Major, Orion, and the Romantic Heart Constellation).
- **🏊 Infinity Pool Swimming Mode**: Couple can slip directly into the warm turquoise infinity pool water!
- **🔭 First-Person Telescope Viewfinder**: 24x zoom lens with crosshairs and elevation telemetry.
- **📖 Vacation Memories Scrapbook**: Stores captured Polaroid keepsakes with timestamps, captions, and an interactive album drawer.
- **👗 Couple Wardrobe Customizer**: Choose between *Sunset Coral & Navy*, *Alpine Emerald & Sage*, *Royal Sapphire & Rose*, or *Midnight Slate & Pearl*.
- **🎵 Enhanced Procedural Soundboard**:
  - Nature: Songbirds, lake waves, alpine wind, night crickets.
  - Weather: Mountain rain, winter breeze.
  - Activities: Coffee mug clinking, pool water splashes, fireplace pops, telescope clicks, and sleep chords.
